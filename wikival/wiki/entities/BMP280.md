# BMP280

## What It Is

A **MEMS digital barometric pressure sensor** from Bosch Sensortec.
The most common barometer chip on Betaflight FCs as of 2026, used
primarily to estimate altitude during [[GPS Rescue]] landings.

## Role in Betaflight Ecosystem

Sits on the FC's I²C (sometimes SPI) bus and is sampled at a low rate
(tens of Hz) compared to the IMU. The pressure → altitude conversion
happens in firmware.

Driver: `src/main/drivers/barometer/barometer_bmp280.c`.

## Key Facts

| Fact | Value |
|------|-------|
| Vendor | Bosch Sensortec |
| Type | Digital barometric pressure sensor |
| Bus | I²C (default) or SPI |
| Pressure range | 300–1100 hPa (≈ -500 m to +9000 m) |
| Resolution | 0.16 Pa (≈ 1.3 cm at sea level) |
| Voltage | 1.71–3.6 V |
| Package | 2.0 × 2.5 × 0.95 mm, port on top |
| BF status | Tier-1, default baro chip |

## Sibling Chips

- **BMP180** — older predecessor, lower precision, less common today.
- **BMP388** — newer, higher precision, used on H7 boards.
- **MS5611** — TE Connectivity competitor, sometimes preferred for
  altitude-hold builds.
- **DPS310** — Infineon competitor, found on some newer boards.

## Installation Notes (carried from [[Barometer (Altitude Sensing)]])

- The **port on top of the package** must remain open to air.
- **Do not conformal coat** over the port — it permanently disables
  the sensor.
- **Open-cell foam** over the chip dampens prop-wash pressure spikes.
