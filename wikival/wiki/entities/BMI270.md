# BMI270

## What It Is

A **6-axis MEMS IMU** from **Bosch Sensortec**. Bosch's flagship
"smart" IMU, designed for wearables and phones — and adopted by the
FPV world as the cheapest available drop-in for the discontinued
[[MPU6000]].

## Role in Betaflight Ecosystem

When MPU6000 supply dried up around 2021–2022, BMI270 was the chip
manufacturers could actually buy at volume. It is now extremely
common on budget and mid-range BF FCs.

Driver: `src/main/drivers/accgyro/accgyro_spi_bmi270.c`.

## Key Facts

| Fact | Value |
|------|-------|
| Vendor | Bosch Sensortec |
| Type | 6-axis IMU (gyro + accel) |
| Max gyro sample rate | **3.2 kHz** ← key tradeoff |
| Bus | SPI / I²C |
| Voltage | 1.71–3.6 V (typical 3.3 V on BF) |
| BF status | Tier-1 supported |
| Common defaults | 3.2 kHz / 3.2 kHz gyro/PID loop |

## Sample Rate Tradeoff

Lower max rate (3.2 kHz vs MPU6000's 8 kHz) means:

- **Pro**: very clean signal, lower aliasing, less noise at the gyro
  output stage. Less filtering needed in firmware.
- **Con**: cannot run 8k/8k looptime configurations. Maxes at 3.2k/3.2k.

The BF community is roughly split on whether MPU6000 8k or BMI270
3.2k flies better at the cutting edge — both are tier-1 in practice.
