# ICM-42688-P

## What It Is

A **6-axis MEMS IMU** from Invensense (TDK), part of the ICM-42xxx /
ICM-20xxx family of "next-generation" Invensense IMUs that succeed
the [[MPU6000]]. The "-P" variant is the precision-optimized SKU.

## Role in Betaflight Ecosystem

Filled the MPU6000 supply gap on higher-end boards from ~2021
onwards. Initially had a mixed reputation — early BF support had
filter defaults that didn't match the chip's noise profile, producing
worse flight feel than MPU6000.

> [!key-insight]
> Per the official BF docs: *"performance on par with or exceeds
> MPU6000 since version 4.4.1."* The 4.4.1 release re-tuned filter
> and sample-rate defaults for ICM-42xxx, which closed the gap.

Driver: `src/main/drivers/accgyro/accgyro_spi_icm42605.c` /
`accgyro_spi_icm42688p.c`.

## Key Facts

| Fact | Value |
|------|-------|
| Vendor | Invensense (TDK) |
| Type | 6-axis IMU (gyro + accel) |
| Max gyro ODR | **8 kHz** (32 kHz via FIFO) |
| Bus | SPI (up to 24 MHz) |
| Voltage | 1.71–3.6 V |
| BF status | Tier-1 since 4.4.1 |
| Gyro full-scale | up to ±2000 dps |

## Family Siblings

- **ICM-20602** — older 6-axis, common on F4 boards
- **ICM-20689** — performance-tier predecessor of ICM-42688
- **ICM-42605** — slightly lower-end sibling, also tier-1 in BF
